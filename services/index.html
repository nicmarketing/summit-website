Summit Marketing offers six core revenue systems: Lead Response System (5-minute automated follow-up), AI Receptionist (24/7 voice and chat AI), Local SEO and AEO (Google and AI engine rankings), Reputation Management (automated review generation), Web Design and Conversion Optimization (starting at $300), and Social Media and Content management. An AI Receptionist is a 24/7 automated voice and chat system trained on your business — your services, pricing, service area, and availability. When a customer calls after hours, the AI answers, qualifies the lead, answers FAQs, and books appointments into your calendar. It turns calls you would have missed into booked appointments around the clock.
When a new lead comes in from your website, Google, or a form submission, our system sends a personalized SMS and email automatically within 5 minutes. The system then follows up persistently until the lead books or opts out. Responding within 5 minutes dramatically increases the chance of making contact versus waiting 30 minutes or more.

Summit Marketing optimizes for both — traditional rankings and AI engine citations — so you capture leads from every search channel.
Web design at Summit Marketing starts at $300 for a Trailhead site — a fast, conversion-focused website for businesses that need a professional online presence quickly. Monthly website management, updates, and landing pages are included in the Ascent ($1,997/month) and Peak ($3,250/month) retainer plans.
