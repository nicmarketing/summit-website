The Future of Search

SEO vs AEO — and why both matter

Google still processes 8.5 billion searches a day — but AI is changing how people find answers. Businesses that win the next decade will be visible in both the old search and the new one.

Traditional
SEO
Search Engine Optimization — ranking in Google's 10 blue links
  • ↗User types a query, gets a list of links
  • ↗Click-through rate drives your traffic
  • ↗Optimized through keywords, backlinks, technical health
  • ↗Powered by Google, Bing, Yahoo
  • ↗Still drives the majority of web traffic today
  • ↗Results take 3–6 months to compound
VS
★ The Future

Answer Engine Optimization — being the answer AI gives
  • ✓User asks an AI, gets a direct answer — your business cited
  • ✓No click needed — AI recommends you by name
  • ✓Optimized through schema, structured content, llms.txt
  • ✓Powered by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overview
  • ✓40%+ of Gen Z uses AI as their first search step
  • ✓Early movers gain a compounding visibility advantage
